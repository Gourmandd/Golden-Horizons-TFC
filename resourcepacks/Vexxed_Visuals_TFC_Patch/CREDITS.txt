
# Modpacks credits

The purposes of this resource pack is to port some of the textures and models from older version of Vexxed Visuals to newer versions.

This resourcepack is a  modified version of a resourcepack created by Vexxel called Vexxed Visuals on Curseforge
It was published under the EUPL.

Curseforge Link: https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/texture-packs/terrafirmacraft-vexxed-visuals
EUPL: https://eupl.eu/
